仕組みHow it works

Three steps from words to video.

No camera, no studio and no editing. Once Yatta knows your face and your voice, a video is a paragraph away.

一

Write 書く

Type a script of up to five minutes, or upload or record the audio yourself.

二

Choose 選ぶ

Pick a face from your avatars and a voice: your own clone, a stock voice, or a free local one.

三

Share 届ける

Yatta renders on its own GPU. Download the MP4, or publish a link that previews in iMessage and WhatsApp.

方式Engines

Two ways to bring a face to life.

Choose realism or speed for each video. Both run on the same queue, and Yatta only starts when the GPU is free.

写

Realistic

LatentSync 1.6
Recommended

Keeps real footage of you and regenerates only the mouth, frame by frame, to match the speech. What viewers see is you.

  • You provideA 15–60 s clip
  • TrainingNone
  • Render timeAbout 13 s per second of speech
  • Best forNatural, true-to-life videos
速

Fast

GaussianTalker
3D Gaussians

A 3D Gaussian avatar trained on a few minutes of you talking. It re-animates the whole face from the audio in seconds.

  • You provide3–6 minutes of video
  • TrainingOne run, started from the studio
  • Render timeAbout 20 s per video
  • Best forQuick drafts and custom backgrounds
声Voices

Say it in your own voice, or any other.

Every video can use a different voice. Preview a voice before you render, and switch whenever you like.

私

Your own voice

Cloned from 10–15 seconds of you speaking, with F5-TTS. It runs on the same machine as the studio.

共有Sharing

One link. It plays anywhere.

Publish a video and Yatta gives it a public page of its own. Everything else in the studio stays private.

  • Rich previews in iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack, X and LinkedIn.
  • Plays in any browser, with nothing to install and no sign-in.
  • Unpublish at any time and the link stops working.

設計Built API-first

Everything the studio does, you can script.

The web app is one client of a versioned JSON API. It has an OpenAPI 3 schema at /api/v1/openapi.json, and a typed TypeScript client is generated from it.

FastAPIPydanticReact 18TypeScriptVitesystemdCloudflare Tunnel 
# Render a video
POST /api/v1/renders
{
  "avatar":  "my-avatar",
  "feature": "latentsync",
  "engine":  "latentsync",
  "voice":   { "provider": "clone", "id": "my-voice" },
  "text":    "Hello from Yatta."
}

# Poll it, then share it
GET  /api/v1/renders/{id}          → queued · voicing · rendering · done
POST /api/v1/renders/{id}/publish  → { "watch_url": "/watch/…" }
同意Consent

Your likeness belongs to you.

Yatta makes avatars and voice clones only of people who have agreed and are 18 or older, and it asks for that confirmation every time. Shared videos are opt-in, one at a time.
